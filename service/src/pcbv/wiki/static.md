# Static electricity

Walking across a carpet can charge you to several thousand volts. You feel a spark only above about
3000 V, but much less can damage a chip. A board can die and you notice nothing.

## Cheap habits that work

- Before you handle a board, touch something grounded: a radiator, a water pipe, or the metal case of a
  plugged-in PC.
- Hold boards by their edges, not by the chips or the pins.
- Work on a wooden desk or on cardboard. Do not work on a carpet or a plastic sheet.
- Keep boards in the anti-static bag they came in, or in a cardboard box. Do not use a plastic bag.

## If you do more of this

An ESD mat with a wrist strap, both connected to ground, costs about 20 EUR. It removes the guesswork.
