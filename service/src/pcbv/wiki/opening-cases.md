# Opening cases

Screws, plastic clips, or both, hold a router case closed. Most damage happens when you force a clip while a hidden screw is still in.

## Find the screws first

- Look under the rubber feet. Peel one corner back with a fingernail. The feet usually go back on.
- Press on the labels. A dip means a screw is under the label. If you cut a label, you usually void the warranty.
- Look for screws in the ventilation slots or behind a removable stand.

## Release the clips

- Use a plastic spudger or an old bank card. A metal screwdriver scratches the case and can slip into the board.
- Start at a corner and work along the seam. Listen for the clicks.
- If a side does not give, look for one more screw before you push harder.

## Take the board out

- Note how the board sits and where the cables go. Take a photo before you unplug anything inside.
- Antenna cables end in small snap-on connectors (U.FL). Lift each connector straight up with a fingernail or tweezers. Do not pull on the cable.
- Lift the board by its edges.
